# Shopify trend-store launch skill

Use `prompts/launch-store.md` as the operating procedure. Treat it as the source of truth for the workflow and approval gates.

Before taking any action, inspect `artifacts/` and resume from the first incomplete section. Keep every decision and external result in the corresponding artifact. Be candid about provider capability, cost, account requirements, and any work that needs the merchant to perform in a browser.

Do not purchase a domain, create or edit a live catalog, publish a Shopify theme, connect a production domain, or enable paid services without the merchant's explicit confirmation in the current conversation.
